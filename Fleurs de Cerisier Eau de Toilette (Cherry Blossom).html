Fleurs de Cerisier Eau de Toilette (Cherry Blossom)

10 ml, RM110.00
50 ml, RM330.00

Delicate flowers, fresh red berries, soft musky notes.

The cherry tree is part of the Provencal landscape.
It is widespread in the Apt region.
This tree changes colors with the seasons, a source of inspiration for the artists: pure white in spring, bright red in summer, soft green in autumn.... The Cherry Blossom Roll-On Eau de Toilette Intense offers all the delicacy and softness of this fruity floral fragrance in a concentrated formula perfect for fragrant touch-ups throughout the day.
Contains a cherry extract from the Luberon region of southern France. 

INCI list

For optimum diffusion, apply your Eau de Toilette on your pulse points - neck and wrists - these are particularly warm spots of the body. To perfume the clothes, spray at a distance of 10 cm.
