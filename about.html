We are the cultivators of nature

Since 1976, L'Occitane has been researching, producing, and selling beauty, skincare, and wellness products with the goal of sharing the wonders of nature with the world. We believe that true beauty goes beyond what the eyes can see.

From seed to skin, every aspect of our operations is thoughtfully considered. We nurture nature to embrace a better future. L'Occitane has always been a cultivator of change. We do our utmost to drive positive transformation and are committed to inspiring those around us to take similar action.

Year after year, we cultivate change

Let us tell you the story of L’Occitane through the journey of a year…

Each year, we plant ingredient crops that are designed to live in harmony with their surrounding ecosystems. We rely on the gifts of Mother Earth and marvel at nature’s power as the plants bloom and bear fruit.

We explore the treasures hidden within these natural crops and dedicate ourselves to scientific research until we achieve breakthrough innovations. These discoveries are then transformed into delightful experiences.

We work closely with local producers because we believe that every action—no matter how big or small—has the power to make a difference.

Follow L’OCCITANE’s inspiring story andembark on a journey of sustainability.

We cultivate natural ecosystems

We believe in sustainable agriculture and develop ecological farming methods to cultivate organic ingredient crops. These ingredients are sourced sustainably to ensure they do not put pressure on precious ecosystems and the natural environment.

We discover the miracles of nature

We understand that, just like other elements in nature, hair and skin have their own ecosystems. To support their regeneration, we have assembled a team of experts who continuously explore and develop new advancements and breakthroughs.

We create beautiful experiences

By harmonizing the five senses, we create a unique sensory experience. Through our distinctive formulas, we craft lasting and precious memories that bring more poetry into people’s lives. L’Occitane’s products are perfect gifts for loved ones because they cherish timeless memories.

We act locally

The world needs change, and for change to happen, everyone’s participation is essential. We understand that our employees, customers, and partners alike can make these changes happen every day.
We cultivate locally, but the actions we take have the power to make the world a better place.
We start with the following commitments: reducing waste, supporting vision care, and respecting biodiversity.

Our brand promise

L’Occitane has six core commitments. We focus on protecting our precious planet and treating everyone with respect.
